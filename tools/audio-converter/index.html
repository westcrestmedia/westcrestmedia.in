Are my audio files uploaded to a server?
No. Everything runs locally in your browser using the FFmpeg WebAssembly engine. Your audio never leaves your device — it is decoded, converted and encoded entirely on your machine.
How many files can I convert at once?
Up to 20 files, each up to 100MB. They convert one by one in a queue — download each result individually or grab the whole batch as a ZIP.
What input formats are supported?
MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, M4A/M4B, ALAC, OGG/OGV, OPUS, WMA, WV, APE, AC3, DTS, AMR, AIFF/AIFC, CAF, MKA, GSM, WEBM, 3GP, TAK, TTA, SHN, W64, VOC, SPX, AU, MPC and more — around 39 formats in total.
What output formats are available?
Fourteen reliable formats: MP3, AAC, M4A, ALAC, Opus, OGG Vorbis, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, MKA, WMA, AC3, AMR-NB and TTA. Lossless formats like FLAC, ALAC and WAV preserve full quality.
Why does the first conversion download ~33MB?
The FFmpeg WebAssembly engine (~33MB) is fetched once so conversions can run 100% offline and private in your browser. After the first load your browser caches it in memory for the whole session.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes, the tool is fully responsive. On phones you can pick audio from your files or music app — either by tapping the drop zone or dragging files in.
